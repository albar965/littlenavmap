# OpenAIRAC Map — Chart Viewer & Browser Guide

This document describes how to browse, view, pin, and integrate open aeronautical charts in **OpenAIRAC Map**.

---

## 1. Opening the Charts Dock

To open the Charts panel in OpenAIRAC Map:
- Go to the main menu: **Window** -> **OpenAIRAC Charts**
- Or use the keyboard shortcut or toolbar icon.

---

## 2. Browsing Airport Charts

1. Type an airport identifier (e.g. `KJFK`, `LFPG`, `KLAX`) into the search bar and press **Find Charts**.
2. Charts are organized into clear category tabs:
   - **All Charts**: Complete published plate inventory.
   - **Airport**: Airport diagrams (APD / ADC), parking & docking (APDC), ground movement (GMC), and hot spots.
   - **Departure (SID)**: Standard departure procedures and takeoff minima.
   - **Arrival (STAR)**: Standard arrival routes.
   - **Approach**: Instrument approach plates (ILS, RNAV/GPS, VOR, NDB, Visual) and alternate minima.
   - **Minima & Info**: General legends and textual procedures.

---

## 3. Chart Viewer Features

- **Built-in Document Display**: View high-resolution vector PDF approach plates directly.
- **Zoom & Fit**: Zoom in/out, fit to page width, fit full page.
- **Rotation**: Rotate 90° clockwise/counterclockwise for landscape charts.
- **Night Presentation Mode**: Inverts chart background for low-light cockpit usage.
- **External PDF Viewer**: Open the plate in your system PDF reader (Acrobat, Preview, Okular).
- **Outdated Warnings**: If a chart belongs to an expired AIRAC cycle, an amber warning banner is prominently displayed.

---

## 4. Pinned Charts Bar

Pin frequently used plates (e.g. Departure SID, Destination Airport Diagram, Approach Plate) to the quick-switch bar at the top of the viewer for instantaneous one-click toggling during critical flight phases.
